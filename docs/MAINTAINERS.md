# Maintainers and Governance

This document outlines the governance model for the Stegnar project, including the roles and responsibilities of maintainers and the process for making decisions.

## 1. Roles and Responsibilities

### Core Maintainers

Core maintainers are responsible for the overall health and direction of the project. Their responsibilities include:

-   Reviewing and merging pull requests.
-   Triaging and managing issues.
-   Guiding the architectural vision of the project.
-   Making decisions on technical and strategic issues.
-   Managing releases.
-   Upholding the project's code of conduct.

### Contributors

Contributors are community members who have made one or more significant contributions to the project. Anyone can become a contributor by having a pull request merged.

## 2. Decision-Making Process

-   Decisions are made through consensus whenever possible.
-   For technical decisions, maintainers will seek input from the community through GitHub issues and discussions.
-   If consensus cannot be reached, the core maintainers will make a final decision.

## 3. Becoming a Maintainer

We are always looking for active and dedicated community members to become maintainers.

### Process

1.  **Become a contributor:** Start by making significant contributions to the project, such as submitting high-quality pull requests, participating in discussions, and helping other users.
2.  **Express interest:** If you are interested in becoming a maintainer, please let one of the current maintainers know.
3.  **Nomination and vote:** A current maintainer can nominate you to become a new maintainer. The existing maintainers will then vote on the nomination. A majority vote is required for approval.

### Qualities of a Good Maintainer

-   A solid understanding of the project's architecture and goals.
-   A history of making valuable contributions to the project.
-   A commitment to the project's success.
-   The ability to work well with others and foster a positive and inclusive community.
